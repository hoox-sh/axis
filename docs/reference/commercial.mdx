# Commercial & Pro SaaS

## Abstract

AXIS is **AGPL-3.0-only**. Two paid tracks sit beside the free self-host:

1. **Commercial** (available) — one-time AGPL escape hatch for closed use.
2. **Pro SaaS** (upcoming) — hosted `axis.hoox.sh` so you skip ops.

## Track 1 — Commercial (available now)

**€349 one-time · per organisation · lifetime 2.x line · Polar · receipt = proof.**

## Which track?

* Embedding AXIS closed-source or hosting it for third parties without AGPL duties → **Commercial**.
* Want hosted charts, sync, and streams without operating Workers → **Pro SaaS** (when live).
* Both → buy both; they are orthogonal (licence ≠ hosting quota).

## FAQ

**Is self-host free?** Yes — AGPL-3.0-only, forever. Paid tracks are convenience (SaaS) or closed-use rights (Commercial).

**Does Commercial include SaaS quota?** No. Hosting seats/evaluate capacity are Pro SaaS only.

**VAT?** Polar is Merchant-of-Record. **Refunds?** 14-day EU digital-goods withdrawal via Polar unless service began.
